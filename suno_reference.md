## Suno Prompting Guide

### Prompting Formula

The most effective prompts often follow a structure that describes the overall feel, genre, and specific instrumentation. A good formula is:

`[Overall Vibe], [Genre], [Instrumentation], [Vocal Style], [Theme/Mood]`

"Hindi lyrics.Modern,bittersweet Bollywood,sarangi, filmi,romantic duet about separation and longing for unification and faithfulness,loyalty,hopefulness,finally reuniting even if for a brief moment.Intertwined vocal harmonies and ad-libs.Soaring and lush orchestral strings.Tabla and traditional Indian instrument accents.Harmonized female and male vocals.Call and response.Dynamic range.Female range is from mezzo-soprano to soprano.

### Lyric Structure Tags

You can guide the AI by using common structural tags in your lyrics:

- `[Verse]`
- `[Pre-Chorus]`
- `[Chorus]`
- `[Bridge]`
- `[Guitar Solo]`
- `[Instrumental]`
- `[Outro]`

### Useful Style Tags

- **Genres:** `pop`, `rock`, `acoustic`, `lo-fi hip hop`, ` Bollywood`, `filmi`, `orchestral`, `electronic`, `folk`
- **Moods:** `sad`, `happy`, `epic`, `romantic`, `bittersweet`, `hopeful`, `melancholic`
- **Vocals:** `male singer`, `female singer`, `duet`, `falsetto`, `choir`, `harmonized vocals`, `ad-libs`
- **Instruments:** `piano`, `acoustic guitar`, `electric guitar`, `synth`, `808 bass`, `strings`, `tabla`, `sarangi`
- **Production:** `lo-fi`, `high-quality`, `studio recording`, `dynamic range`, `lush`, `soaring`
